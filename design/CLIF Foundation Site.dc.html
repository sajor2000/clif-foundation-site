What CLIF is

A shared format for ICU records

Ventilators, infusions, labs and vital signs are stored differently at every hospital. CLIF puts them in one common layout so the same question can be asked at any site. It is open source and free to use.

Why a Foundation

The Consortium does research. The Foundation would help hospitals adopt.

The CLIF Consortium is a network of academic centers that built the standard and runs studies with it. Getting a new hospital started takes training, technical help and maintenance that research grants do not cover. The Foundation would fund that work.

What a gift funds

Training, implementation help and upkeep

Gifts would support courses for clinicians and data teams, hands-on help for hospitals converting their records, and maintenance of the open specification and tools. No gift funds research or touches patient data.

01 — How CLIF research works

Data stays at each hospital. Code travels.

The CLIF Consortium conducts federated research: each site converts its EHR data to CLIF locally, runs shared analysis code, and returns only aggregate results. The Foundation’s proposed work helps more sites reach that first step.

02 — How CLIF organizes ICU data

Life support, as connected tables.

Ventilation, vasoactive infusions, renal replacement, and ECMO are recorded by different devices and systems. CLIF gives each its own table, timestamps every row, and links them all to the same hospitalization.

01 / mission

Make the CLIF standard practical for any hospital to adopt.

Draft mission statementproposed

CLIF Foundation is an Illinois nonprofit corporation formed to support wider adoption of the Common Longitudinal ICU data Format, an open-source standard for organizing critical care EHR data.

Its proposed work focuses on what helps a new institution get started and keep going: clear education, practical implementation help, and long-term care of the open specification and tools.

Two organizations, distinct roles

CLIF Consortiumexisting
  • Conducts federated, privacy-preserving multicenter research
  • Network of clinician-scientists, data scientists, and academic medical centers
  • Publishes the data dictionary, ETL guide, and open-source tools
  • clif-icu.com ↗
CLIF Foundationproposed
  • Would support adoption through education and implementation help
  • Would sustain maintenance of the open specification and tools
  • Does not conduct research or hold patient data

Four proposed programs, all aimed at helping hospitals use CLIF. None are funded or operating yet; gifts would start them.

Who is behind it

CLIF was built by the CLIF Consortium, a network of clinician-scientists and data scientists at academic medical centers. CLIF Foundation was incorporated in Illinois to carry out adoption work that sits outside the Consortium’s research mission.
