01Brand position

The CLIF Foundation is a proposed nonprofit that supports wider adoption of the Common Longitudinal ICU data Format. The CLIF Consortium conducts federated research and maintains the standard. The two share the CLIF symbol and keep distinct visual identities: the Consortium uses its crimson logo; the Foundation uses a one-color logo with a single signal-lime accent.

02Logo and lockup

The lockup is the original CLIF symbol, a 1px rule, and “Foundation” set in Funnel Display 600. Use of the CLIF logo is approved by the Consortium.

  • Minimum logo height 24px on screen, 0.25in in print. Clear space on all sides equals one node of the symbol.
  • Never recolor the logo petrol or mint, place it on a mint ground, stretch it, or add effects.
  • The crimson original appears only where the Consortium itself is credited, such as joint documents.
  • Production files: request official one-color SVG artwork from the Consortium. Current previews apply a filter to the PNG.

05Space, layout, shape

  • Spacing scale on a 4px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 120.
  • Layout: content max 1424px; gutters clamp(16px, 4vw, 56px); section padding clamp(72px, 9vw, 120px); reading measure 680px.
  • Shape: 2px tags, 3px controls, 6px panels and figures. No shadows in product UI.
  • Rules: lists and tables open with a 1px ink rule; rows divide with 1px rule-200; the active nav item carries a 2px ink underline.
  • Controls: 36px (desktop chips), 44px default and touch minimum, 48px primary actions.
  • Responsive: fluid from 320px; flex rows wrap; header collapses to a Menu button below 1000px.

09Dark mode

Dark mode is a token swap, not a second design. Every inline style reads a semantic variable, declared once for light (:root) and once for dark ([data-theme="dark"]). Inverse bands stay dark in both modes.

  • "Existing" tags invert to a white fill with ink text; "proposed" tags keep the green fill.
  • The logo inverts to white via the same filter token; the crimson original never appears.
  • Theme follows the system by default; the header toggle overrides and persists.

09Writing and content

  • Plain, factual, third person for the organization; “we” only in direct calls to act.
  • Conditional voice for anything not yet real: “proposes to”, “would support”, “published when adopted”.
  • Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. No exclamation points, no emoji.
  • Calls to action name the outcome: “Email a gift inquiry”, “Start with CLIF 101”.
factual boundaries
  • Programs and board appointments are proposed; label them.
  • Credit research to the CLIF Consortium and original investigators, with links to the source.
  • No invented biographies, partnerships, statistics, funding commitments, tax-exempt status, or deductibility claims.
  • Giving is by inquiry to info@clificufoundation.org; no payment checkout.

10Accessibility

  • All text pairs meet WCAG 2.2 AA; most meet AAA.
  • Focus: 2px outline in the text color with 3px offset; green on ink grounds. Every interactive element.
  • Skip link first in tab order; aria-current on the active page; Menu button uses aria-expanded and closes on Esc.
  • Touch targets 44px minimum, 48px for primary actions, 8px between targets.
  • External links marked ↗ with a hidden “opens external site” label. Color transitions only; respects reduced motion.
